Case board: a colour + icon per kind of lead

#245 · reported by the owner at the S4 test-deploy playtest, 10 Nov · sibling of #246 (compact player chips)

The problem

Every lead is the same manila row: R-badge, Pangolin title, one grey line. Seven kinds of evidence look identical, so you read every title to find “the meetings” or “what happened in the Vault”.

The tone

A detective's case file, not a dashboard. Kind colour works like coloured file dividers and highlighter tabs: muted, hatched, printed slightly off the ink line. It sorts the eye; it never shouts. The ⚠ band stays the loudest thing on the board.

Guard rails

  • Colour never alone: always icon and kind name (name also in the row's accessible name).
  • Avoid --clay (pins, primary) and --bad (⚠ band). Text stays ink on light tints.
  • Tokens in theme.css only; shown in /dev/gallery; pnpm lint:theme clean.
  • No extra row height at 360 px; 44 px targets unchanged.
